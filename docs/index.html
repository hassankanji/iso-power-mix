Storage here is discharge only - energy sent out of batteries and pumped hydro, with charging excluded because it is load, not generation. It is not the net battery position, and it never goes below zero. The sources disagree on this (some report net, some report discharge, some report nothing), and mixing them is what previously put EIA's national line far above ISOs sitting below zero. Only ISOs that publish a storage series appear.

Hourly national generation from EIA-930's lower-48 aggregate - the hourly shape the other tabs cannot show, since they carry whole settled days. This is not real time: EIA publishes the fuel-type breakdown roughly 12-18 hours after the hour ends, so the newest bar here is normally late yesterday evening. The exact lag is printed next to the headline number. Values are average MW over the hour, shown as GW. Nothing is fetched until you open this tab or press Refresh.

This tab calls the EIA API straight from your browser using this site's own EIA key. If that key is ever rejected or rate-limited, you can put your own free key in here instead - register here (instant, email only). It is stored in this browser's local storage and never leaves it except in requests to api.eia.gov.
