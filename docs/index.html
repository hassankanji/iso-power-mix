How to read this →

One line per ISO for the selected fuel, plus the US lower-48 total (EIA) for national context. Use "% of each ISO's total" to compare mix share across markets of very different size.

Battery here is discharge only - energy sent out of the fleet, with charging excluded because it is load, not generation. It never goes below zero, and it is not the net battery position. Pumped hydro is not in this bucket; every ISO reports it inside Hydro, so EIA's pumped storage is filed there too.
The national line is a floor, not a total. CAISO, PJM and NYISO report no battery series to EIA-930 at all, so EIA's lower-48 figure omits the largest battery fleet in the country. Expect it to run below the ISO lines rather than above them - that is a reporting gap at the source, not a measurement. ERCOT, SPP and ISO-NE have no battery column in their own feeds either, so their lines come from EIA.

Total daily generation contributed by each ISO. The stacked total here equals the fuel-type total on the National Trend tab for the same dates.

Each area is compared against its own latest day shifted back, not against a fixed calendar date, because the ISOs publish on different delays. Hover a change to see the exact day it is measured against.
